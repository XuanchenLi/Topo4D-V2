Towards High-fidelity 3D Talking Avatar with Personalized Dynamic Texture

Xuanchen Li     Jianyu Wang     Yuhao Cheng    
Xingyu Ren     Yanping Li     Xiaokang Yang     Yichao Yan‡
MoE Key Lab of Artificial Intelligence, AI Institute, Shanghai Jiao Tong University
(‡Corresponding author)
Paper

Given calibrated multi-view videos, our scan-free 4D reconstruction method can faithfully capture the performer’s head pose, expression and gaze.

Abstract

Production-level digital humans require not only emotional facial expression dynamics but also realistic head pose and gaze to achieve lifelike presence. However, traditional capture pipelines are costly to reconstruct facial details and struggle to acquire pose and gaze simultaneously. These pipelines rely on complex workflows involving specialized operation, high computational overhead, and labor-intensive post-processing, making them unsuitable for large-scale production. To address these limitations, we introduce a scan-free 4D reconstruction framework that simultaneously captures topology-consistent expression dynamics, pose, and gaze from calibrated multi-view videos. Our approach decomposes the task into a prior-guided multi-stage tracking process. By integrating multi-source priors at varying granularities, including coarse model-based priors for initialization and fine-grained Gaussian-derived priors for refinement, we effectively reduce cumulative errors and mutual interference among pose, expression, and gaze. Furthermore, to capture the geometry details and address the over-smoothing issue, we design a collaborative strategy between a topology-preserving Gaussian set and a detailed non-topological Gaussian set, where the former provides reliable structural and motion priors while the latter captures details. Based on this hybrid representation, we propose a normal-guided detail transfer mechanism that effectively enhances the mesh details. Comprehensive experiments demonstrate that our method outperforms existing approaches in reconstruction quality and robustness.
